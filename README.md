# 📊 Business Insights Dashboard – Executive View

## 📌 Overview

The **Executive View** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a high-level overview of business performance across revenue, profitability, market share, customers, products, and business divisions.

The dashboard brings multiple business KPIs into a single view, allowing users to quickly identify performance trends, regional opportunities, customer contribution, product performance, and potential business risks.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance
- Track Revenue, Gross Margin, Net Profit and Forecast Accuracy
- Compare current performance with benchmarks and previous years
- Analyze revenue contribution across different divisions
- Monitor market share trends
- Identify top customers and products by revenue
- Analyze regional/sub-zone performance
- Identify business risks such as Out-of-Stock and Excess Inventory

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

The Executive Dashboard tracks the following major KPIs:

- **Net Sales**
- **Gross Margin %**
- **Net Profit %**
- **Forecast Accuracy %**
- **Revenue Contribution %**
- **Gross Margin %**
- **Net Error %**
- **Market Share %**
- **Risk / Inventory Status**

The dashboard also provides comparisons against:

- Benchmark (BM)
- Last Year (LY)
- Target

---

## 📈 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Key Insights by Sub-Zone

Provides a detailed comparison of business performance across different sub-zones.

Metrics include:

- Net Sales
- Gross Margin %
- Net Profit %
- Revenue Contribution %
- ATLiq Market Share %
- Net Error %
- Risk Status

This helps identify high-performing and underperforming regions and highlights operational risks.

---

### 2. Revenue by Division

Revenue is analyzed across different business divisions, including:

- PC
- P&A
- N&S

The visualization shows each division's contribution to overall revenue.

---

### 3. Revenue by Channel

Revenue contribution is analyzed across:

- Retailer
- Direct
- Distributor

This provides visibility into how different sales channels contribute to overall business revenue.

---

### 4. Yearly Trend Analysis

The dashboard tracks yearly performance across:

- Net Sales
- Gross Margin %
- Net Profit %
- ATLiq Market Share %

This makes it easier to understand long-term business trends and changes in profitability and market position.

---

### 5. PC Market Share Trend

The dashboard compares **ATLiq market share with competitors** over time.

Competitors represented include:

- ATLiq
- BP
- Dell
- Lenovo
- Innova
- Pacer

The visualization helps analyze changes in competitive market positioning over multiple years.

---

### 6. Top 5 Customers by Revenue

The dashboard identifies the top customers based on revenue contribution.

Metrics include:

- Customer
- Revenue Contribution %
- Gross Margin %

This provides insight into customer concentration and profitability.

---

### 7. Top 5 Products by Revenue

The dashboard highlights the highest-revenue products along with:

- Revenue Contribution %
- Gross Margin %

This helps identify important products driving overall business performance.

---

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

- Region

Users can interact with the dashboard to drill into specific business segments and performance periods.
